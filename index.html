A few good questions

Before you
make the leap.

Still finding your format?
Start with what you know.

What is the minimum order quantity?

We have used 5,000 units as a planning example in this preview. Actual minimums depend on size, material, print method and closure, and need to be confirmed in your quote.

Can I request a custom size or spout?

Include your target dimensions, volume and dispensing requirements in the brief. The configurator shows representative starting points; availability needs technical confirmation.

How long does production take?

The sample production window is 3–5 weeks after artwork and specification approval. Sampling, compatibility testing and freight sit outside that example window.

Are all pouches recyclable?

No single answer applies to every pouch. The film, spout, cap, inks and local collection route all matter. Request an assessment for the complete pack and intended market.

Can I see a sample before committing?

Include “sample kit” in your project brief, along with the products and sizes you want to explore. Sample availability, cost and timing would be confirmed by the team.
