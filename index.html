Can’t find your answer?
Ask our team directly.

What can I fill a spout bag with?

Our spout bags can hold liquids, powders and semi-solids for a range of applications.

Which sizes do you list?

Four bags: Spout Side Bag Small (100 × 160 mm), Spout Center Bag Small (90 × 120 mm), Spout Side Bag Medium (140 × 230 mm) and Spout Side Bag Medium with Handle (200 × 250 mm), given as length × width. All use 155 mic film. The nozzle is 10 mm on the two side bags without a handle, 22 mm on the handle bag and 25 mm on the center bag.

Are your spout bags eco friendly?

They’re crafted using environmentally friendly materials, and they use less plastic than rigid containers like bottles.
