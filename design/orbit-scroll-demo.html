Shipping This For Real

Three ways to build the orbit

The scroll math is the same regardless of what's spinning. What changes is what you render at each angle.

01

Turntable photo sequence

Shoot the real pouch on a rotating table — 36 to 72 stills covering a full turn — then swap the visible frame as scroll progress advances. This is what most high-end product pages (Apple included) actually use: it's just an image, so it always looks real.

02

Live 3D model

Import an actual 3D scan or CAD render of the pouch into three.js or a similar WebGL engine, and drive its camera or rotation from scroll position. Heavier to build, but lets you relight, zoom, or swap print designs without reshooting.

03

Hand-rolled 3D

What's running above: a small vanilla-JS renderer that projects a simplified pouch mesh onto a canvas and rotates it with plain trigonometry — no photos, no libraries. Useful for proving the mechanic out before real photography or a 3D scan exists.
