Sustainability

Mono-material, without losing the barrier

Conventional pouches laminate three incompatible plastics together, which is what makes them unrecyclable. Our mono-material line uses a single polymer family, so the finished pouch goes into existing film recycling streams.

Conventional PET / AL / PE Three polymers laminated — not separable, not recyclable.
Mono-material Mono-PE One polymer family — store drop-off recyclable, comparable shelf life.
